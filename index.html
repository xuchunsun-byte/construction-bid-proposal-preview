Who it is for

The sample is for small contractors, subcontractors, and engineering firms preparing a bid presentation for a client, a main contractor, or a public tender. The language is English. Placeholder project details can be replaced with the bidder’s own job, team, and figures. This is a presentation template, not a bid-writing service, and it does not claim to win work on its own.

What is in the 8-page sample

The internal sample contains the eight slides below. Each slide is laid out so a bidder can edit the wording in PowerPoint or Google Slides.

  1. 01

    Cover

    Project title, bidder name, client, and date.

  2. 02

    Understanding the brief

    A structured restatement of the client's requirements and site conditions.

  3. 03

    Approach

    How the work will be organised, supervised, and delivered.

  4. 04

    Bid summary

    Scope of work and a clear commercial summary the presenter can complete.

  5. 05

    Schedule

    A programme view of key stages and milestones.

  6. 06

    Relevant work

    Comparable projects and relevant experience.

  7. 07

    Terms

    Qualifications, exclusions, and conditions of the offer.

  8. 08

    Contact

    The bid team and how the client can follow up.
